# Account Reconciliation Policy

Owner: Group Accounting · Applies to: all balance sheet accounts

> Illustrative policy written for this prototype.

## 1. Purpose

Account reconciliations provide independent evidence that ledger balances are
supported by underlying records. This policy defines preparation, review and
escalation requirements for reconciliations performed during the close.

## 2. Preparation requirements

Every balance sheet account is assigned to a named preparer. A reconciliation is
complete only when the ledger balance, the supporting balance, the difference and
an explanation of each reconciling item are documented. A reconciliation with an
unexplained difference is not complete, whatever its status flag in the close
tool.

## 3. Reconciliation status

The permitted statuses are: reconciled, in progress, open, and not applicable. An
account in status "open" or "in progress" at the point of entity sign-off is an
incomplete reconciliation and is reported in the close package.

## 4. Mismatches and differences

### 4.1 Investigation threshold

A reconciling difference at or above EUR 5,000 must be investigated and
explained before sign-off. Differences below this amount are aggregated and
monitored for trend.

### 4.2 Material differences

A reconciling difference at or above the materiality threshold defined in the
Materiality and Escalation Policy requires review by the entity Financial
Controller and is disclosed to Group Accounting before sign-off. It may not be
written off by the preparer.

### 4.3 Ageing

Reconciling items open for more than sixty days are escalated to the Financial
Controller regardless of amount, because ageing items indicate a process failure
rather than a timing difference.

## 5. Suspense and clearing accounts

Suspense and clearing accounts (account 199000 and equivalents) must be cleared
to zero at each month-end. A non-zero balance at sign-off is escalated to the
entity Financial Controller with an ageing analysis of the residual items.

## 6. Review

Reconciliations are reviewed by someone other than the preparer. Evidence of
review is the reviewer's identity and the review date recorded in the close tool.
An automated recommendation does not constitute review.

## 7. Escalation path

Preparer → entity Financial Controller → Group Accounting → Head of Internal
Audit. Items are escalated one level at a time unless fraud is suspected, in
which case the matter is referred directly to Internal Audit.
