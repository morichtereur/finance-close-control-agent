# Manual Posting Control

Owner: Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR) · Control reference: CLS-04

> Illustrative control description written for this prototype.

## 1. Control objective

Manual journal entries are the principal route by which the financial statements
can be misstated without a corresponding business transaction. This control
ensures that manual postings are justified, timely, supported and reviewed.

## 2. Definition

A manual posting is any entry created directly in the ledger by a user rather
than generated by an upstream sub-ledger, interface or recurring posting
programme. Document types SA, SB and KR created without a source document
reference are treated as manual for the purpose of this control.

## 3. Justification requirement

### 3.1 Standing requirement

Every manual posting must record why a manual entry was necessary. Acceptable
justifications include correction of an interface failure, recognition of an
accrual for which no sub-ledger exists, and reclassification requested by Group
Accounting.

### 3.2 Unsupported manual entries

A manual posting without a supporting document reference is an unsupported
manual entry. Unsupported manual entries must be escalated to the entity
Financial Controller and may not be cleared by the preparer alone. Where the
amount is at or above the second-level approval threshold, the item is escalated
without waiting for the next close review meeting.

## 4. Timeliness

### 4.1 Late postings

A manual posting made more than five calendar days after the document date is a
late posting. Late postings require a documented justification from the preparer
explaining the delay, retained with the entry.

### 4.2 Postings after the close calendar cut-off

Manual postings after the entity cut-off defined in the Month-End Close Policy
require approval by Group Accounting and are reported in the close summary.

## 5. Postings outside normal business hours

### 5.1 Definition

Normal business hours for posting purposes are 07:00 to 20:00 local entity time
on working days.

### 5.2 Treatment

A manual posting outside normal business hours is not itself an error. It is a
risk indicator and is assessed together with other indicators. An out-of-hours
posting that is also unsupported, made to a high-risk account, or at or above the
second-level approval threshold is escalated for review. An out-of-hours posting
that is fully supported, within threshold and to a routine account is documented
and requires no escalation.

## 6. Monitoring and reporting

The Manual Posting Control is monitored monthly. The population of manual
postings, the number of unsupported entries and the number of late postings are
reported to the entity Financial Controller and, in aggregate, to Group
Accounting as part of the close package.

## 7. Prohibited automation

No automated routine may approve, post, reverse or modify a journal entry in the
ledger. Automated analysis may prioritise, classify and recommend; the decision
to act rests with a named person.
