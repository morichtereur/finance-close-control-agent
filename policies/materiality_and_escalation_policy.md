# Materiality and Escalation Policy

Owner: Group Accounting · Aligned with: group audit planning materiality

> Illustrative policy written for this prototype. The thresholds below are
> configuration values in this repository, not accounting guidance.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the quantitative thresholds used during the close to decide
what must be investigated, what must be reviewed by a person and what must be
escalated beyond the entity.

## 2. Materiality thresholds

### 2.1 Group materiality

Group materiality for the current financial year is EUR 250,000. An individual
item at or above group materiality is material to the consolidated financial
statements and is reported to Group Accounting.

### 2.2 Entity performance materiality

Entity performance materiality is 60 percent of group materiality unless Group
Accounting sets a lower entity-specific threshold.

### 2.3 Clearly trivial threshold

Items with an absolute value below EUR 5,000 are clearly trivial. A clearly
trivial item is not escalated on the basis of amount alone. It is still escalated
where it involves a high-risk account, a segregation-of-duties failure, a
suspected duplicate or any indication of irregularity.

## 3. Escalation triggers

### 3.1 Mandatory escalation

The following are escalated to the entity Financial Controller irrespective of
amount:

- an unsupported manual journal entry at or above the clearly trivial threshold
- a posting to a high-risk account without documented second-level approval
- a suspected duplicate posting that has not been cleared
- a segregation-of-duties failure
- a suspense or clearing account with a non-zero balance at sign-off
- any indication of management override

### 3.2 Amount-driven escalation

An item at or above group materiality is escalated to Group Accounting. An item
at or above the second-level approval threshold but below group materiality is
reviewed by the entity Financial Controller.

### 3.3 Variance escalation

A month-on-month movement in an account balance exceeding 50 percent and EUR
100,000 in absolute terms requires a documented explanation. Where no explanation
can be obtained before sign-off, the variance is reported as an open close
exception.

## 4. Risk rating

For the purpose of triage, close exceptions are rated as follows:

- **High** — mandatory escalation applies, or the amount is at or above group
  materiality, or several indicators of irregularity coincide.
- **Medium** — the item breaches a control requirement or exceeds the
  second-level approval threshold, but no indication of irregularity exists.
- **Low** — the item is a documentation or data-quality finding below the clearly
  trivial threshold with no aggravating indicator.

## 5. Human review requirement

Every item rated high must be reviewed by a person before disposition. Automated
triage may propose a disposition; it may not close a high-rated item. Items rated
medium are reviewed by a person where the supporting evidence is incomplete or
where the automated analysis is not confident.

## 6. Aggregation

Items below individual thresholds are aggregated by account and by entity.
Aggregated uncorrected differences exceeding group materiality are reported to
the Audit Committee irrespective of individual item size.
