# Month-End Close Policy

Owner: Group Accounting · Applies to: all reporting entities

> Illustrative policy written for this prototype.

## 1. Close calendar

The close runs from working day one (WD1) to working day five (WD5). Sub-ledgers
close on WD1, accruals and manual entries are posted by WD2, reconciliations are
completed by WD3, entity review takes place on WD4 and entity sign-off is on WD5.

## 2. Cut-off

### 2.1 Entity cut-off

No entry may be posted to the closing period after the entity cut-off on WD3
without approval from Group Accounting.

### 2.2 Period integrity

An entry whose document date falls in a period earlier than the posting period
must state why the item was not recognised in the correct period. Repeated
period-integrity findings for the same entity or preparer are reported as a
process deficiency rather than as individual items.

## 3. Account and cost center integrity

### 3.1 Valid combinations

Postings must use an account and cost center combination that exists in the
approved master data derivation for the entity. A combination that has not been
used by the entity in the preceding twelve months is treated as unexpected and is
confirmed with the cost center owner before sign-off.

### 3.2 Treatment of unexpected combinations

An unexpected account and cost center combination is a data-quality finding, not
by itself a misstatement. It is routed to the preparer for confirmation. Where
the combination also involves a high-risk account or an amount at or above the
second-level approval threshold, it is escalated to the Financial Controller.

## 4. Close exceptions

Exceptions raised by continuous controls monitoring are triaged daily during the
close. Each exception is assigned an owner, a disposition and, where relevant, a
remediation action. Exceptions that remain open at sign-off are listed in the
entity representation letter.

## 5. Sign-off

The entity Financial Controller signs off the close. Sign-off confirms that
reconciliations are complete, that open exceptions are documented and that all
items above materiality have been reviewed by a person.

## 6. Roles

Preparer prepares and documents. Reviewer performs independent review. Financial
Controller owns the entity close and its exceptions. Group Accounting owns policy,
the calendar and consolidation. Internal Audit performs independent assurance.

## 7. Use of automated analysis

Automated analysis may be used to triage, classify and prioritise close
exceptions and to draft recommendations. It does not replace review, does not
constitute evidence of review, and has no authority to post to the ledger.
